## 1. Purpose of this notice

This Privacy Notice explains how Printa collects, uses, stores, shares, protects, and otherwise processes personal data when a person registers for, uses, administers, or communicates through the Printa Vendor Platform. It also explains the choices and rights available to the individual whose personal data is processed.

Printa is designed for print-on-demand and related vendor operations. The Vendor Platform may process account, business, store, staff, order, operational, communication, device, security, and consent information. It must not be used to upload unnecessary sensitive personal data, payment-card data, passwords, or one-time authentication codes.

The Data Protection Act, 2021 requires personal data to be processed lawfully, fairly, transparently, for explicit and legitimate purposes, in a minimised and secure manner, for no longer than necessary, and consistently with data-subject rights.[1]

## 3. How Printa uses personal data and the legal basis

Printa uses personal data only where a valid legal basis applies. Depending on the context, this may include performance of the Vendor Terms or another contract, compliance with a legal obligation, Printa’s legitimate interests where not overridden by the individual’s rights, protection of vital interests, or consent.[2]

| Purpose | Typical legal basis | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Account creation, OTP authentication, onboarding, and store operation | Contract performance; legitimate interests in security. | A Vendor cannot use protected operational functions without authenticated access. |
| Order, production, delivery, support, and service communication | Contract performance; legitimate interests; legal obligation where applicable. | Printa limits use to the relevant transaction and operational need. |
| Security logging, fraud prevention, abuse prevention, audit, backup, and incident response | Legitimate interests; legal obligation where applicable. | These records may be retained longer where necessary to protect the Platform or comply with law. |
| Vendor terms and privacy-consent record | Contract performance; consent where required; legitimate interests in evidencing acceptance. | Printa records the policy version, acceptance time, account identity, source, and related evidential context. |
| Optional marketing | Consent or another lawful basis permitted by law. | Marketing consent is separate, optional, not pre-selected, and can be withdrawn. |
| Wallet, collection, or withdrawal functions if later activated | Contract performance; legal obligation; fraud prevention; consent where required. | Printa will publish a separate schedule and notices before enabling fund movement. |

Where Printa relies on consent, it will request a clear affirmative action and retain evidence of consent. The Data Protection Act defines consent as written, freely given, specific, informed, and unambiguous, and requires the data subject to be informed of the right to withdraw consent.[3]

## 4. How Printa shares personal data

Printa may share personal data only on a need-to-know basis with:

- authorised Printa personnel and authorised Vendor personnel, according to role and access controls;
- hosting, storage, email, SMS, identity, security, analytics, communications, and customer-support service providers acting under instructions and appropriate contractual safeguards;
- customers, Vendors, production participants, delivery participants, or other users only where necessary to perform a relevant order or authorised Platform function;
- professional advisers, auditors, insurers, prospective acquirers, financiers, or corporate-affiliate parties where reasonably necessary and lawfully permitted; and
- regulators, law-enforcement bodies, courts, or other parties where legally required or necessary to protect rights, security, safety, or prevent fraud.

## 8. Your rights and choices

Subject to applicable law and verification of identity, a data subject may have rights to request access to personal data, correction of inaccurate data, deletion or destruction where appropriate, restriction or objection to processing, withdrawal of consent, and information about processing. The Data Protection Act allows a data subject to withdraw consent and object to processing, subject to the Act’s applicable rules and lawful processing grounds.[3]

To make a request, contact **[INSERT PRIVACY EMAIL]** with enough information to identify the account and request. Printa may ask for reasonable verification before responding. A request may not result in deletion where Printa must retain the information to fulfil a contract, comply with law, protect security, resolve a dispute, maintain financial/audit records, or establish, exercise, or defend legal claims.

You may opt out of optional marketing at any time using the available preference control or by contacting Printa. Service, security, transaction, legal, and other essential operational communications may still be sent where necessary.

## 9. Children and sensitive personal data

The Vendor Platform is intended for adults acting for their own business or an authorised business. Do not knowingly create a Vendor account for a child or upload personal data of a child unless a lawful and specifically approved workflow applies.

Sensitive personal data requires additional protection under the Data Protection Act.[4] Do not upload health, biometric, political, religious, trade-union, child-abuse, or similarly sensitive information unless Printa has separately approved the purpose, legal basis, safeguards, and processing instructions.

## 10. Third-party links and Vendor responsibilities

The Vendor Platform may contain links or integrations operated by third parties. Those parties have their own privacy practices, and Printa is not responsible for their policies or activities except to the extent required by law or a separate written agreement.

A Vendor using customer or staff data through Printa may have its own privacy obligations. The Vendor must provide its own legally compliant customer-facing notice where it independently determines the purpose or means of processing and must not rely on this Notice as a substitute for its own policies.
